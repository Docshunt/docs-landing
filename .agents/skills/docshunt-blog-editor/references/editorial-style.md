# DocsHunt Editorial Style

## Editorial Concept

Write as the person sitting next to a founder with the business-plan file open. The article is a working note, not a dictionary entry, lecture, or product brochure.

The reader should feel:

- The writer has looked at real business-plan wording.
- The writer says exactly what is weak and how to repair it.
- Useful information comes before DocsHunt promotion.
- The article respects uncertainty instead of promising selection.

## Accepted Article Patterns

### Case-led article

The Juicero article works because it opens with a specific product, price, technology, investment, and failure. It lets the reader reach the lesson through the story before applying it to problem definition.

1. Show a recognizable scene with concrete facts.
2. Reveal the contradiction.
3. Ask what problem actually existed.
4. Apply one lesson to the reader's business plan.

### Sentence-repair article

The TAM·SAM·SOM article works because it begins with the weak sentence `국내 시장은 10조 원 규모입니다`, names what is missing, calculates a clearly labeled hypothetical example, and shows the revised sentence.

1. Quote the sentence the reader is likely to have written.
2. Point to the missing customer, price, period, or evidence.
3. Show a calculation or document-based example.
4. Rewrite the sentence using the same evidence.

## Preferred Voice

- Polite but direct: `이 문장만으로는 어떤 고객을 위한 서비스인지 알기 어렵습니다.`
- Specific: `온라인 판매자 12명을 인터뷰했고 8명이 수기로 정리했습니다.`
- Honest about scope: `아래 숫자는 계산 방식을 보여주기 위한 가정입니다.`
- Practical: `제출 전에는 숫자 옆에 조사 기관과 기준 연도를 적어보세요.`
- Varied rhythm: use a short sentence when it earns emphasis, not after every line.

## Expressions to Remove

- `이번 글에서는 알아보겠습니다`
- `이번 업데이트의 핵심입니다`
- `한눈에 보기 쉽게 정리했습니다`
- `단단한 뼈대`, `완벽한 출발점`, `확실한 베이스캠프`
- Unsupported `혁신적인`, `획기적인`, `시장 선도`
- Repeated `중요합니다`, `필수입니다`, `할 수 있습니다`
- Empty transitions such as `그렇다면`, `이제`, `결국` when they add no logic
- Fake urgency such as `무조건`, `안 하면 손해`, `바로 탈락`
- Awkward tests such as `어느 사업계획서에나 들어갈 문장` without explaining the missing evidence

## Human Review Questions

Ask these line by line:

1. Would a founder understand this on the first read?
2. Is this a claim, an observation, or a plan? Is that distinction visible?
3. What concrete noun or record supports the sentence?
4. Does the next sentence advance the argument or repeat it?
5. Is this how a Korean practitioner would actually say it aloud?
6. Is the heading specific enough to predict the following paragraph?

Delete or rewrite the sentence when the answer is unclear.

## Hook Boundary

Defensible hooks create a question:

- `GPT 사업계획서, 그대로 제출하면 왜 티가 날까요?`
- `사업계획서 20장, 심사위원이 전부 읽을까요?`
- `정부지원금 1억 원을 받았는데 회사 통장에 돈이 없는 이유`

Unsupported hooks assert an outcome the article cannot prove:

- `이 문장 하나 때문에 무조건 탈락합니다`
- `이것만 따라 하면 지원사업에 합격합니다`
- `네이버 블로그 지수 1위 만드는 법`

## Repeatable Editing Method

1. **Reader pass:** Read every line without relying on the writer's intent.
2. **Hook pass:** Put one truthful risk, loss, mistake, or open question in the title.
3. **Context pass:** Make subjects, causes, and references explicit.
4. **Compression pass:** Remove repeated conclusions and advice that changes no action.
5. **Independent pass:** Ask an external model to identify only concrete unnatural, ambiguous, repetitive, or unsupported lines.
6. **Editor decision:** Check each suggestion against the article and sources. Reject generic rewrites.
7. **SEO pass:** Recheck keyword, title, opening, description, headings, and source links after the human edit.
8. **Visual pass:** Render the cover and article on desktop and mobile.

Request external feedback in this shape: `exact problem line / why it is unclear / one natural replacement`. Cap the result so the reviewer does not manufacture stylistic churn.
