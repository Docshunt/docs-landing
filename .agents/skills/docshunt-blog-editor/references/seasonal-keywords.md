# Seasonal Keyword Priorities

Use this reference when choosing what to write or rewrite next. It ranks topics; it does not replace checking the current official notice.

## Why the season matters

DocsHunt readers search most when a support-program notice is open and its deadline is close. A post that answers a notice-specific question (eligibility, required documents, evaluation items, deadline) before or during the notice window is worth more than an evergreen explainer published after the window closes.

Plan the calendar backward from the notice window:

| When                                | What to publish or refresh                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| October–December                    | Preparation posts for next year's packages: evaluation items, business-plan structure, evidence to collect, last year's notice marked as `[지난 공고]` |
| January–March                       | Notice-specific posts as soon as each official notice is posted: dates, eligibility, amounts, required documents, common application mistakes          |
| Within the final 7 days of a notice | Short task guides: submission checks, K-Startup submission status, document format questions                                                           |
| After the deadline                  | Mark the post as a past notice, keep the evergreen parts, and point to the next expected cycle without inventing dates                                 |

## 2027 changes are not final

The Ministry of SMEs and Startups briefing on the 2027 government budget proposal (policy briefing `newsId=156775671` on korea.kr) said several pre-startup programs, including 예비창업패키지 and 도전! K-스타트업, would be consolidated into 모두의 창업. This is a budget proposal before National Assembly approval.

- Write it as `2027년 정부 예산안 기준 계획` and never as a confirmed program change.
- Do not state that a specific program receives zero budget unless an official document says so; cite it beside the sentence.
- Do not delete or redirect posts about programs that may be consolidated. Update them with a dated note when the final notice appears.
